Hi! I'm a PhD researcher at the University of York's Institute for Safe Autonomy, where I'm part of the Centre for Doctoral Training in Safe AI Systems (SAINTS). I've worked on using interpretability to make latent reasoning models monitorable and auditable, and on untangling why LLMs are sycophantic.

Right now my focus is on safety assurance for advanced AI systems. I'm interested in the places where existing ideas break down, and understanding how these problems relate to Agent Foundations research. If you'd like the full picture, here's my resume.
